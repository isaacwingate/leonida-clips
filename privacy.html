Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy covers the Leonida Clips uploader, a private tool that the owner of the Leonida Clips accounts uses to upload our own video clips to our own accounts.

Who uses it

Only the account owner. The tool is not offered to the public and nobody else signs in to it.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (scope youtube.upload): uploads our clips to our own channel, with a title, description and tags. It cannot read, change or delete anything else on the channel.
  • Google Drive (scope drive.file): creates one folder and uploads our clips and their caption text files to it. It can only see files it created itself.
  • TikTok (scope video.upload): sends our clips to our own TikTok inbox as drafts.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and are used only to perform the uploads above. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, we do not use cookies or analytics on this site, and we do not use Google or TikTok data for advertising or to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions or TikTok's Security and permissions settings. Removing access stops all uploads; nothing else is kept.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @l3onidaclipz on TikTok or YouTube.
